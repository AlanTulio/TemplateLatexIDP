\begin{abstract}
This preliminary research project addresses institutional airfare purchase planning at the intersection of Public Administration and Data Science. It proposes integrating historical purchase records, public data and prospective quotations to characterize fare variation, purchase lead-time ranges and uncertainty across selected routes. Observed purchases alone do not establish the counterfactual price of the same flight at another purchase date. The planned analytical prototype will therefore support administrative judgment rather than claim guaranteed savings or causal effects. This document is a teaching skeleton adapted from the author's preliminary project; data collection, model evaluation and empirical conclusions remain future research activities.
\end{abstract}
